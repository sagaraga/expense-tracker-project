Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expenses you log, including amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We do not collect payment card details or bank credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to run the service: to show your expenses, build your spending summaries, and keep your account secure. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your information is stored in our database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards, and passwords are stored in hashed form. We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and you may ask us to delete it at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page is displayed. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data corrected or deleted, please get in touch with the Spendly team at support@spendly.example.
